About GreenSteps Initiative

Empowering communities through environmental action

GreenSteps Initiative is a fictional environmental organization dedicated to promoting community-driven sustainability efforts. Our mission is to connect volunteers with local cleanup events and provide tools for effective coordination and tracking.

Through the EcoCleanUp Hub, we aim to:

  • Make cleanup events easily discoverable
  • Streamline volunteer registration and coordination
  • Track and showcase environmental impact
  • Build a stronger, greener community
